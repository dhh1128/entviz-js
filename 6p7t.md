- 2026-08-04T04:49Z Full write-up: reviews/security-scan-2026-07-31/RESULTS.md F3 (characterize.ts:106) and F9 (EntvizPill.ts:307). Sibling finding in the python reference, tracked separately there: render() DOES cap at 64 KiB, but at the cap a base58 input costs ~703 ms measured, against the ~14 ms the cap's own rationale claims. Fix shape is shared — replace the digit-at-a-time BigInt accumulation with ceil(len * log2(base) / 8), which is all sizeBits needs.
- 2026-08-06T06:37Z Fixed in fc048ff. Correction to the fix shape recorded above: ceil(len * log2(base) / 8) was EVALUATED AND REJECTED, not adopted. sizeBits is spec-normative (docs/spec.md Resolution A) and the estimate disagrees with the exact value whenever leading digits are zero — a Bitcoin P2PKH address measures 192 bits exactly and 200 by the estimate; a base58 core of all '1's measures 8 and estimates 160. It would change rendered output and break conformance. Shipped instead: the reference's balanced divide-and-conquer fold (entviz 77d35cc), same integer, 740 ms -> 17.5 ms at the 64 KiB cap, plus memoized combine exponents and a hex-based bit length. The cap is now enforced at the top of characterize() and at the top of classifyInput before parse() (both arms), which covers every public route to the decode. Conformance stayed 104/104.
